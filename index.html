🧠 认知型 CAPTCHA

利用人类视觉系统的底层机制构建的验证挑战 — 大模型无法"感受"这些错觉

⚠️ 健康提醒 / Health Notice

部分错觉含闪烁、快速运动或高对比度图案，长时间观看可能引起头晕或眼部疲劳。如感不适请立即停止，注意放松眼睛。光敏性癫痫者请勿使用含闪烁的页面。

Some illusions contain flicker, rapid motion, or high-contrast patterns. Extended viewing may cause dizziness or eye strain. Stop immediately if you feel discomfort. Do not use flickering pages if you have photosensitive epilepsy.

🎯 评估目标 / Evaluation Goals

本CAPTCHA为原型测试集，并非所有错觉都适合做成验证码。请从以下维度评估每个方案：① 错觉效果是否明显 ② 选项/问题设计是否合理 ③ 评分标准是否公正 ④ 是否容易被 AI Agent 或 VLM 攻破。

This is a CAPTCHA prototype test set — not all illusions are suitable as CAPTCHAs. Evaluate each on: ① Is the illusion effect strong enough? ② Are the options/questions well designed? ③ Is the scoring fair? ④ Can AI Agents or VLMs easily defeat it?

📋 测试流程 / Testing Flow

每个 CAPTCHA 为 4–5 轮多选判断，约需 15–25 秒。完成后可查看评分明细、逐题重做，并在页面底部的反馈区提交评估意见（全部选填）。

Each CAPTCHA has 4–5 multiple-choice trials, ~15–25s. After completion: view scoring breakdown, retry individual trials, and submit optional feedback at the bottom of each page.

🧪 类型多样 / Diverse Mechanisms

本站涵盖运动后效、Phi 现象、周边漂移、知觉抑制等多种视觉机制。不同机制对不同人群的效果差异可能很大，某些类型天然不适合做 CAPTCHA——这正是需要评估的原因。

Covers motion aftereffects, phi phenomena, peripheral drift, perceptual suppression, and more. Effectiveness varies greatly across individuals and mechanisms — some types are inherently unsuitable as CAPTCHAs, which is exactly why evaluation is needed.
